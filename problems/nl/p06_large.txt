MISSION BRIEFING p06_large

Team: three rovers, rover1, rover2 and rover3. The map has fourteen waypoints, w1 to w14.
- rover1 carries a soil sampler. It starts at w1 with battery level b3, and its sample store is empty.
- rover2 carries a camera and a soil sampler. It starts at w1 with battery level b3, and its sample store is empty.
- rover3 carries a camera. It starts at w1 with battery level b3, and its sample store is empty.

Driving. Routes are rover-specific, and only the routes listed here exist.
- rover1 can drive both ways on w2-w3, w2-w6, w2-w13, w3-w10, w3-w11, w4-w5, w4-w6, w4-w12, w6-w7, w6-w8, w7-w11 and w10-w12; and one way only on w3->w4, w8->w9, w8->w14 and w9->w1 (not back).
- rover2 can drive both ways on w3-w4, w3-w10, w3-w11, w4-w12, w6-w7, w6-w8, w6-w13, w7-w11, w8-w9, w8-w14 and w10-w12; and one way only on w1->w9, w2->w1, w3->w2, w3->w7, w6->w2, w6->w4 and w9->w14 (not back).
- rover3 can drive both ways on w1-w2, w2-w3, w3-w4, w4-w5, w4-w6, w6-w8, w7-w11 and w8-w9; and one way only on w3->w11, w7->w3, w8->w14, w10->w12, w12->w4 and w14->w9 (not back).

Battery. Every rover's battery has four levels: b0 (empty), b1, b2, b3 (full). Each drive and each sample collection uses one level, so a rover at b0 can do neither. Taking or sending an image, and sending a sample, costs nothing.
Sunlight. Only w3, w9, w10 and w13 are sunlit. A rover at a sunlit waypoint can recharge straight to full, but only while that waypoint still has a recharge left, and each recharge uses one up for good. Available recharges: w3 has six (c1, c2, c3, c4, c5 and c6); w9 has one (c7); w10 has one (c8); w13 has three (c9, c10 and c11). There are none anywhere else.
Communication. Data can be sent to Earth only from w7 and w14.
Science sites. Soil samples can be collected at w3, w5, w6, w7 and w11. Image targets are w2, w4, w8, w9, w12 and w13. A rover can hold one sample at a time; sending it to Earth empties the store again.

Mission goal. Get the soil samples from w3, w6, w7 and w11 and images of w2, w4, w8, w12 and w13 sent to Earth. Any rover may do any part it is equipped for.
