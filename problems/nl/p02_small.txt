MISSION BRIEFING p02_small

Team: one rover, rover1. The map has seven waypoints, w1 to w7.
- rover1 carries a camera and a soil sampler. It starts at w1 with battery level b3, and its sample store is empty.

Driving. Routes are rover-specific, and only the routes listed here exist.
- rover1 can drive both ways on w1-w3, w3-w4, w3-w6, w4-w5 and w5-w7; and one way only on w1->w2 and w2->w7 (not back).

Battery. Every rover's battery has four levels: b0 (empty), b1, b2, b3 (full). Each drive and each sample collection uses one level, so a rover at b0 can do neither. Taking or sending an image, and sending a sample, costs nothing.
Sunlight. Only w2 and w4 are sunlit. A rover at a sunlit waypoint can recharge straight to full, but only while that waypoint still has a recharge left, and each recharge uses one up for good. Available recharges: w2 has one (c1); w4 has two (c2 and c3). There are none anywhere else.
Communication. Data can be sent to Earth only from w7.
Science sites. Soil samples can be collected at w2, w4 and w7. Image targets are w3, w5 and w6. A rover can hold one sample at a time; sending it to Earth empties the store again.

Mission goal. Get the soil samples from w2 and w4 and images of w3 and w5 sent to Earth. Any rover may do any part it is equipped for.
